# Roadmap

1. Bootstrap, documents, deterministic galaxy, browser saves, repair introduction, flight, surfaces and stations.
2. Simulation: emergencies, medical status, space/ground combat, docking, EVA and salvage.
3. Economy, contracts, crafting, upgrades, fleet and customization.
4. Five chapter campaign, phased bosses, endings, codex and free play.
5. Automated rule tests and browser smoke tests, mobile QA, deploy verification.
6. Expand depth after first release: physical boarding crew combat, multi-stage contracts, more event choices, resource containers, terrain collisions, richer audiovisual assets and real-device Safari validation.
