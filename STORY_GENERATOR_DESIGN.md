# Story generator design

A free captain in 2497 makes a living in the Reach. The Lattice is a background mystery. Legendary threats and the network investigation are optional; finishing one arc leaves the director and local world running.

ORION balances pressure and recovery, MORRIGAN varies rhythm within survivable constraints, HESPER favors travel/social mystery. The director uses ship/medical/resource conditions, recent crises, reputation and unresolved threads. It chooses eligible cards, not a periodic unconditional pirate spawn. State and seeded draw index are saved.

Cards create situations; existing repair, medicine, flight, cargo and combat decide outcomes. Resolutions record specific choices and consequences, create NPC memories and potential delayed callbacks. Do not roll direct death or narrate actions that never occurred.

Lore has provenance: observed fact, official account, rumor, restricted evidence and unresolved question. Calypso emptied in 2472; why WELCOME BACK appeared remains unknown. The Choir is a human dream network, not a proven civilization or final cosmic answer.

Chronicle keeps bounded significant events and named participants, not per-frame movement. Save-specific stories should arise from system collisions. Meaningful 1/5/20-hour tests must check conditions, repetition and callback lifecycle, not simply count random strings.
