# STARFALL: VOID FRONTIER

## Vision

Russian-language single-player space survival RPG. A compact but complete campaign lives in a seeded galaxy, with free exploration after the ending. Static hosting and mobile play are first-class requirements.

## Loop

Wake aboard the damaged Kestrel. Restore its reactor and seal the hull. Pilot with inertia, scan contacts, align and dock at a station, trade and take contracts. Land on a planet, mine and investigate ruins, return with cargo, craft supplies and install technologies. Travel to increasingly dangerous regions to defeat five guardians of the ancient Choir network. Decide whether to destroy, control or entrust the network.

## World and progression

Five regions with five systems each. Ten planet biomes, stations, derelicts, anomalies and resources are deterministic from the seed. Persistent depletion and defeated enemies prevent repeated free loot. Difficulty belongs to regions, not player level. Five chapter bosses use distinct attacks and phases. Credits, salvage, reputation and research fund progression.

## Survival

Six body parts, wounds, blood, pain, oxygen and radiation. Ship compartments track hull breaches, fire and module integrity. Repair, fire suppression and medical items consume resources. Defeat permits recovery at a station with a credit penalty rather than destroying the campaign.

## Presentation

Procedural Canvas 2D, dark industrial panels, cyan instrumentation, amber mission markers, restrained bloom. HTML menus sit above a continuously rendered world; opening a menu pauses simulation. Touch sticks, 44px controls, safe-area padding, reduced effects and capped pixel ratio.

## Delivery scope

The original brief is a long-term target. Each shipped system must provide a usable loop. PROGRESS.md records precise coverage and gaps; avoid presenting unimplemented depth as finished.
