# Rework plan — 2497 / The Reach

The supplied MASTER_REWORK.md and LORE_BIBLE.md supersede the former short-campaign design. This is an incremental rework of the running game, not a claim that the hundred requested systems are already complete.

## Audited KEEP

Canvas/DOM architecture, seeded geometry and IDs, fixed-step movement, medical body parts, projectile collisions, guarded wreck boarding, cargo accounting, real routed contracts/escort, local checksummed saves and Pages deployment. Preserve prior saves and the five threats as an optional legacy world arc.

## Audited REWORK

Instant repair/search, instant dock-to-station transport, identical station rooms/NPC mannequins, remote shop/clinic buttons, obligatory five-boss HUD and technology gates, random-event timer, automatic SOS payouts and encyclopedic certainty about the Choir. Establish physical range, duration, pressure/sealing and named contact requirements before expanding simulation.

## REMOVE from the new expedition

The mandate to save the galaxy and defeat five sequential levels. Early lore spoilers and statements that the origin/function of the Lattice are proven. No fictional crew, scheduled NPCs or simulated economies represented only by labels.

## Ordered implementation

1. Unified contextual, timed, cancellable physical interactions and persistent significant history.
2. Permission, docking envelope, magnetic capture, sealing/pressure, connected ship/tunnel/station traversal, generated station identity/layout and nearby services.
3. Named persistent NPCs, actual movement, memories and physically present crew with injuries/permanent death.
4. Compartment atmosphere/power/damage and repair tools.
5. ORION/MORRIGAN/HESPER director choosing eligible story cards from condition and recent history.
6. Threads and callbacks referring to real earlier actions.
7. Coarse factions, station stock/production and news from mutations.
8. Physical freight and contracts; 9. surface regions/settlements; 10. art/audio; 11. long progression; 12. 1/5/20-hour balance simulations.

Each stable checkpoint must pass typecheck, tests, production build, production browser acceptance and publish to main. PROGRESS.md records the exact achieved subset and next task.
